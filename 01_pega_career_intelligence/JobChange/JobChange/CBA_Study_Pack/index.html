How to use this pack

About the worked example. Chapter 01 follows Lending Care, built for a fictional New Zealand bank, Tōtara Bank. Its people, accounts, topics, thresholds and architecture are invented for teaching and are not CBA's or ASB's. Chapter 01 §1.0 lists exactly which facts are public and citable; everything else is design reasoning you present as your own.
  1. Read the interview dossier first: the org, the people, the process, your fit, pay and level.
  2. Then read Chapter 01 in order. The walkthrough (§1.8) is the part to rehearse aloud.
  3. Finish with §1.15: draw the five-minute whiteboard from memory, then answer the twelve prompts out loud.
