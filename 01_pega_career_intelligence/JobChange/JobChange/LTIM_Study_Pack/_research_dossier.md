### The three things that must be said honestly

1. **"Customer Service" on his resume is almost certainly not the Pega Customer Service
   strategic application.** At MBFS it reads as custom-built case management serving a
   customer-service business function, in the same breath as Collections, Debt Recovery and
   Remarketing. A Pega CS interviewer will ask about the Interaction Portal, interaction
   wrapper cases, service request types and composites within two minutes. If he implies
   framework experience he does not have, the interview ends there. Chapter 02 must give
   him the exact sentence that separates the two.
2. **He has zero Constellation hands-on and zero React hands-on.** The JD asks for
   Constellation DX API on Infinity 24x. This is a real gap, not a framing problem.
   Chapter 03 must be written as "conceptual, with a credible ramp plan", never as
   experience.
3. **He is on 8.x, the JD says 24x.** Honest line: he knows the platform, not the release.

### The strongest card — say this early in any LTIM interview

JD-A asks, verbatim, for "**PEGA CSSA** ... with hands-on experience in Infinity 24x with
Constellation DX API **KAFKA queue processors** with 4-5 years experience."

He is a CSSA with **4.5 years**, **50+ Kafka topics** and **Queue Processors at production
scale**, including a replay/recovery API. On three of the five named requirements he is an
unusually exact match, and Kafka-plus-Pega is a genuinely scarce combination in this market.
The two he misses are Constellation DX API and the 24.x release. That is the whole
conversation — lead with the match, name the gap, present the ramp plan.
