## What makes a team worth choosing

Compare the actual product and production ownership, engineering practices, integration depth, learning opportunity, location, on-call expectations and approved compensation. Public sources cannot settle manager quality, workload, retention or team culture. Employee moves, welcome posts and event attendance do not establish replacement vacancies.

For each priority role, the next useful facts are the exact team/client allocation, grade, approved total CTC and fixed-pay split, requisition status, location/remote terms and joining window. No outreach or applications were sent.
