📚 The 30-Day Deep Prep Plan
If you have a month, you can actually internalise this stuff instead of cramming. Plan: ~1 hour/day
weekdays, 3 hours each weekend day. About 35 hours total.
Week 1 — Foundations and the 50 Most-Asked - Days 1–2: 01 Top 50 end to end. Solve every
question on paper before reading the answer. - Days 3–5: 04 Chapter 1 (fundamentals) + Chapter 2
(joins). All 50+50 questions. - Weekend: 04 Chapter 3 (windows + CTEs). This single chapter is where
most candidates lose the offer. Don't rush.
Week 2 — Performance and reliability - Days 6–8: 04 Chapter 4 (indexes + optimization). Slowest
chapter — go deep. - Days 9–10: 04 Chapter 5 (transactions, isolation, locking). Memorise the
isolation tables from 07. - Weekend: 05 Scenario Library — debug + optimize categories (all questions).
Week 3 — Breadth, schema thinking, and modern stack - Days 11–12: 04 Chapter 6 (normalization)
+ Chapter 7 (aggregations). - Days 13–14: 04 Chapter 8 (set ops) + Chapter 9 (stored procs). - Day
15: 04 Chapter 10 (modern features). - Weekend: 06 Modern DB Specifics — your target warehouse
end to end, plus skim the others.
Week 4 — Scenarios, intel, dress rehearsal - Days 16–18: 05 Scenario Library — design schema +
what-will-this-return + index decisions categories. - Days 19–20: 05 — transaction conflicts + data
quality investigations. - Day 21: 02 Top 200 at Indian MNCs — read like a magazine, mark the patterns.
- Day 22: 03 What Changed in 2026 — talk-track material for "what's new?" questions. - Days 23–25:
Re-attempt every question you flagged across the month. - Days 26–28: Mock-interview drill — pick 15
random questions from 04 + 10 from 05, time yourself 4 minutes each, speak the answer out loud. - Day
29: 07 Cheatsheet — re-read until you can recite the function patterns and index pitfalls without looking.
- Day 30 (interview eve): Read 07 Cheatsheet + skim 01 Top 50. Sleep early. Eat properly.
Optional: read 08 Salary Map the day before negotiation, not before the technical round.

How To Use The Cheatsheet On Interview Day
07 Cheatsheet is 1 page. Treat it like an exam aid, not study material.
Offline interview: print it on A4, fold in half, keep in your bag. Glance at it in the waiting room or
auto on the way. Do NOT carry it into the panel room.
Online interview: pin the PDF open on your second monitor, or print and tape next to your webcam
— angled so you can glance without breaking eye contact. Interviewers don't mind; many of them
have their own notes too.
Just before the round: read it once, top to bottom, slowly. The three isolation-level tables are the
highest-leverage thing on the page — most candidates fumble READ COMMITTED vs REPEATABLE
READ under pressure. Memorise the row order.
During the round: if the interviewer asks "which isolation level prevents phantom reads?" — you
should answer in 2 seconds without consulting anything. The cheatsheet is for confidence-of-recall,
not lookup.
