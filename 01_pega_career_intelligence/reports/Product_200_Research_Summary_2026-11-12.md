## Evidence rules

1. **Current employer requisition** in Bengaluru and Pega in essential duties: candidate opening.
2. **Employer-hosted role that mentions Pega only as nice-to-have**: adjacent opening, not a Pega developer seat.
3. **Mirrored job, recruiter post, indexed employer page, dated profile join**: research lead only; ask for an employer req and check payroll/location.
4. **Career homepage loads or search has no results**: never proof that a 200-account employer has no vacancy across all recruiting sites.
5. A job post or public profile cannot establish the present Pega version, named squad allocation, precise Bangalore headcount, reporting line, or a guaranteed interview.

The 200-row table is the coverage answer; the five employer-verified Pega-targeted requisitions are the application answer. The remaining accounts need a working, first-party searchable portal or direct requisition before a live-hiring conclusion is possible.
