## Practical outreach rule

Use this order:

1. Official role ID first.
2. Confirm role says Pega / PRPC / CSSA / case management / integrations.
3. Confirm location and experience band.
4. Contact one verified TA/recruiter, not a whole list.
5. Attach a three-line fit: CSSA, Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, Kafka/REST/SFTP/SQL, collections/case management.
