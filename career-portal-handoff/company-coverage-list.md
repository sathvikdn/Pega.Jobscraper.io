# Company coverage: original list versus Pega Radar

Snapshot of the saved configuration and original coverage records, prepared 15 November 2026. No live portal checks were performed for this report.

**How to read this list**

- **Company-specific LinkedIn**: a dedicated `pega <company>` search is configured. This is a keyword search, not a verified LinkedIn company-ID filter.
- **General search only**: broad Pega searches may find this employer. There is no dedicated employer query.
- **Workday / Oracle link**: a direct career-board adapter is configured. It does not guarantee every posting or subsidiary is covered, or that the last request succeeded.
- **No direct adapter**: this employer’s career portal is not directly scanned by the new radar.
- **Missing dedicated entry**: absent from the new registry after name reconciliation; no dedicated LinkedIn query or portal adapter. A job might still surface in general searches.

The original report has **286 target entries**, including duplicates and naming variants, and **178 distinct saved portal URL strings**. These are not counts of unique companies or successfully searched portals.

The new registry has **200 companies**, including **135 product/GCC employers**, **68 direct product/GCC portal adapters**, and **42 product/GCC employer-specific LinkedIn queries**.

Original-list reconciliation: **239 entries mapped to 130 current registry employers**; **39 entries form 37 missing-name groups**; **8 entries form 8 related/ambiguous groups requiring review**. Missing-name groups combine punctuation and trailing “India” variants only.
